Dr. Ray Islam (Mohammad Rubyet Islam)

PhD (USA) | MASc (Canada) | MASc (UK) | MBA (UK) | B. Tech (BD) | Masters (ALM) - In progress (USA)

AI Scientist Technology strategist Author Educator Speaker Reviewer Entrepreneur

Dr. Ray Islam is an AI scientist and global technology leader specializing in artificial intelligence, machine learning, cybersecurity, and enterprise transformation. His career bridges academia, industry, and public service, with a focus on translating emerging technologies into practical, high-impact solutions.

He is the author of Generative AI, Cybersecurity, and Ethics (Wiley), catalogued by more than 500 universities and libraries worldwide, including Harvard, MIT, Yale, Cambridge, Oxford, and the University of Toronto. The book was featured at Black Hat, and Saudi Arabia’s Institute of Public Administration (IPA) has partnered with Wiley to translate it into Arabic and publish an initial 1,000 copies by 2028. Dr. Islam has earned academic degrees and worked across five countries on three continents, completed the MIT MicroMasters in Statistics and Data Science, and is currently pursuing an ALM in Finance at Harvard University.

He has served as a professor and held AI, data science, and strategic leadership roles with organizations including Deloitte, Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, Booz Allen Hamilton, American Institutes for Research, and Carrefour. His professional experience spans finance, healthcare, energy, supply chain, manufacturing, defense, government, and technology, providing him with a broad cross-industry perspective on the application of AI and advanced analytics. His work has supported organizations including the White House, U.S. Department of Defense, U.S. Air Force, NASA, U.S. Space Force, Department of Justice, Department of Commerce, GSA, FEMA, and Fiserv. He has architected and developed multiple AI solutions that are integral to operational and decision-support functions across U.S. government, military, and private-sector organizations. Dr. Islam has also founded AI and technology ventures and created educational programs and Udemy courses to prepare data scientists and data analysts. He has been recognized as an honored listee of Marquis Who’s Who for contributions to Education and Science & Technology, part of a biographical tradition spanning more than 125 years and noted for its influence on prominent figures such as Warren Buffett.

His work is guided by a commitment to trustworthy, explainable, secure, and socially beneficial AI. His research interests include artificial intelligence, ethical finance, and quantum computing.
