Ray Islam (Mohammad Rubyet Islam)

PhD (USA) | MASc (Canada) | MASc (UK) | MBA (UK) | B. Tech (BD) | Masters (ALM) - In progress (USA)

AI Scientist Author Educator Speaker Reviewer

Dr. Ray Islam is an AI scientist, author, educator, entrepreneur, and technology strategist whose work spans artificial intelligence, machine learning, responsible AI, cybersecurity, and enterprise transformation. His career bridges scientific research, higher education, industry, and public service, with a focus on translating emerging technologies into practical solutions for complex organizational and societal challenges.

He is the author of ***Generative AI, Cybersecurity, and Ethics*** (Wiley), which has achieved broad international academic reach and has been catalogued by more than 500 universities and libraries worldwide, including Harvard, MIT, Yale, Cambridge, Oxford, and the University of Toronto. The book was featured at **Black Hat**, and Saudi Arabia’s **Institute of Public Administration (IPA)** has entered into an agreement with Wiley to translate it into Arabic and publish an initial 1,000 copies by 2028. IPA contributes to developing public-sector leadership and institutional capabilities supporting Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030.

Dr. Islam has earned academic degrees and worked across five countries on three continents, bringing a global perspective to science, technology, education, and leadership. As part of his commitment to lifelong learning, he completed the MicroMasters in Statistics and Data Science from MIT and is currently pursuing graduate studies (ALM) in Finance at Harvard University, further integrating artificial intelligence, statistics, data science, and quantitative finance.

Across academia and industry, Dr. Islam has served as both a professor and technology leader, holding AI, data science, and strategic positions with organizations including **Deloitte, Lockheed Martin, Raytheon, Booz Allen Hamilton, American Institutes for Research, and Carrefour**. His work has supported major organizations across finance, healthcare, insurance, manufacturing, retail, defense, government, and the public sector. His public-sector work has included initiatives supporting the White House, U.S. Department of Defense, U.S. Air Force, NASA, U.S. Space Force, U.S. Department of Justice, U.S. Department of Commerce, GSA, FEMA nand others, as well as major financial-technology organizations such as Fiserv. His contributions have included AI strategy, data science, advanced analytics, technology modernization, and AI-enabled solutions.

Beyond institutional leadership, Dr. Islam has founded AI and technology ventures and developed educational programs focused on preparing data scientists and data analysts. He has also created and published courses on Udemy, extending access to practical education in AI, data science, and emerging technologies to a global community of learners. These efforts reflect his commitment to expanding technical education, promoting lifelong learning, and developing the next generation of technology professionals.

Dr. Islam has been recognized as an honored listee of Marquis Who’s Who for contributions to Education and Science & Technology, joining a biographical tradition established more than 125 years ago to document distinguished contributors across science, education, business, and public service. The publication’s enduring influence has been acknowledged by prominent figures including Warren Buffett, who has spoken about the formative value of learning from the lives and experiences of accomplished individuals profiled in Who’s Who.

At the center of Dr. Islam’s work is a commitment to advancing AI that is trustworthy, responsible, explainable, secure, and beneficial to society. His interdisciplinary career across research, education, enterprise innovation, entrepreneurship, and public service reflects a broader mission to strengthen the connection between scientific advancement and real-world impact.
